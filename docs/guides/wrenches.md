# Wrenches and You

Some blocks have the ability to be manipulated via the **Wrench from Create**.
This mod also implements this feature for the RedRouter Block and Animatronics.

## Right Click
By right clicking on the blocks upwards or downwards facing side, you can **rotate them horizontally**.
Both the RedRouter and Animatronic can be rotated in **90° steps**.

## Shift + Right Click

By holding shift while right clicking, both the **RedRouter Block and the Animatronic can be removed** and immediately put **in your inventory instantly**.
